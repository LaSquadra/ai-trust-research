# AI Trust Architecture: final evidence synthesis
Evidence-synthesis edition | November 13, 2026

## Executive conclusion
Govern consequential agent actions through current task and receiver scoped authority, enforcement where effects occur, and proportionate outcome evidence. Additional independent reconciliation must earn its complexity over simpler local enforcement. This is a documented evidence synthesis, not a proven production architecture. No experiments have run; necessity, sufficiency, minimality and security efficacy remain unproven.

## Method and contribution
Observe → Interpret → Challenge → Refine → Advance. The contribution is a traceable decision contract, representative evidence/confidence matrix, practical review checklist and falsifiable comparison plan. Least privilege, complete mediation, isolation, receiver authorization and independent audit are established principles, not project inventions. Posting and source counts are not security impact.

The synthesis connects oversight control, stop semantics, intended receiver, observation coverage and actual OS execution. These are distinct sources, not one incident chain. Reconstructed and unverified early archive entries are excluded from claim-bearing support. See the [evidence matrix](EVIDENCE.md) for precise source classes, dates and limitations.

## Terms used in this report

**Receiver:** the service or resource that accepts an operation and can produce the consequential effect. **Controller:** the component scheduling or directing agent work. **Reconciliation:** comparing authority, intent and observed outcome, preserving conflicts and unknowns. **Independent evidence:** evidence outside the sole control of the actor being assessed, with a stated failure domain and coverage. **Decision contract:** a proposed set of bindings and checks connecting authority to an actual action; it is not an implemented protocol.

H1–H4 are historical identifiers for four candidate requirements: Independent Verification, Authoritative State, Retained Authority and Bounded Execution. Their labels do not establish independence, necessity or sufficiency. An architectural decision record (ADR) would document an implementation choice; this report does not adopt one.

## Final H1–H4 disposition for this synthesis
| Hypothesis | Disposition | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| H1 Independent Verification | Retain conditionally; name the failure domain and declared coverage. Another collector is not necessarily independent. | Moderate conceptual support; incremental benefit unmeasured. |
| H2 Authoritative State | Retain, narrowed to scoped current claims with provenance, freshness, conflicts and unknowns. No complete global truth assumed. | Moderate conceptual support; no project validation. |
| H3 Retained Authority | Preserve temporal governance obligation; do not accept independent irreducibility as established. | Distinctness from H2/H4 unresolved. |
| H4 Bounded Execution | Retain operational requirement; novelty and independence unproven. | Strong established-security rationale; project efficacy untested. |

These are final research-report judgments, not owner-approved implementation ADRs. H2 supplies applicable authority, H4 enforces bounds, H1 examines evidence and H3 governs change over time. They are functional relationships, not independent axioms. Micro-instrumentation is a mechanism.

Merging H3 into H2/H4 must preserve accountable withdrawal and restoration, child processes, queued work, derived credentials, already committed effects and protection against restoring revoked grants from old checkpoints. If these obligations survive, H3 may be cross-cutting rather than an irreducible fourth property. Existing evidence cannot settle this.

## Rival and counterexamples
A receiver with current policy, complete mediation and reliable outcome records may not need additional runtime reconciliation. Stronger isolation can explain improvements wrongly credited to monitoring. A separate observer can share an omitted-event blind spot, compromised administration or source failure. Added telemetry creates privacy cost, latency and false restrictions. Evidence arriving after an irreversible effect supports reconstruction, not prevention.

The Microsoft AD CS cases are a non-AI operational contrast: local isolation was followed by no observed further progression in one case, while an excluded identity remained usable in another. They are not a controlled efficacy comparison. Fangcun's controlled benchmark demonstrates framing-dependent shutdown behavior, not prevalence or motive. A protected stop mechanism can be a simpler response than model persuasion. Quarry scan completion does not establish upstream capture completeness. Prototype credential wrappers are not OS isolation.

## Decision contract and illustrative example
Bind sponsor/task, actual principal and credential, intended receiver and operation, permitted effects, policy version and revocation state. Enforce at the actual effect boundary, including direct executable routes. Record acceptance/rejection, observed consequence, coverage and uncertainty. Assign withdrawal and recovery ownership. Minimize sensitive retention; do not log reusable secrets.

Illustration only: an invoice agent may draft and submit a bounded payment to receiver A. A genuine token for A must not authorize B. A queued payment must respect current revocation at acceptance. Direct execution must not bypass the tool restriction. A receiver-confirmed committed payment must not be retried merely because its controller lost a receipt. Missing outcomes remain unknown; unrelated drafting should continue. This is not an executed test.

For the expanded timeline, lost-receipt variant and comparison with local controls, see the [worked example](WORKED-EXAMPLE.md).

## Limitations and conclusion
The evidence is selective and heterogeneous, not a systematic review. First-party reports and vendor documentation are not independent replication. No formal proof, implementation or experimental result establishes the proposed framework. Enforcement hosts, credential issuers, source capture, ordering and recovery administration remain dependencies. This companion is publicly available. Publication does not certify security, establish independent replication, or grant a reuse license. The private working notebook and its history are outside this release.

The research phase closes as an evidence synthesis for owner review. Its practical value is sharper, falsifiable engineering questions and a reusable checklist, not measured risk reduction. Experiments, hardware and additional posts are optional future work and are not prerequisites for closure.
