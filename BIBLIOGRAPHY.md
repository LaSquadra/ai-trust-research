## How to read this list

- The focused [evidence matrix](EVIDENCE.md) identifies sources carrying the closing argument. This broader inventory includes background, competing explanations, reporting and implementation references. Inclusion is not endorsement or proof of relevance to every conclusion.
- Dates below are **dates of citing archive records**, not asserted publication or incident dates. Original dates and source classes for conclusion-bearing evidence remain in the matrix. Undated indexes may cite the same source.
- Verification status for every entry is **archived citation; not freshly revalidated for this bibliography**. Record-level flags identify documents containing reconstruction, retrieval or verification caveats. They are conservative warnings, not claims that each linked source in that document failed verification.
- No missing titles, publication dates or source-specific validation results were invented. Where the archive lacks a usable title, the URL is the label. This inventory is not a systematic literature review or independent replication.
- Images/illustrative media, LinkedIn publication links, ChatGPT artifacts, private-notebook links and credential-bearing URLs are excluded. URL fragments and tracking parameters are normalized for deduplication; substantive query parameters remain.
